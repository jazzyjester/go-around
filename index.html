Go Around!

Privacy policy · last updated 17 November 2026

Privacy

Go Around does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No network connection. The game never sends anything off your device.
  • Your progress and settings (which routes you've finished, parcels collected, and the sound and music options) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, sell or delete. If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated before any new version of the app does anything different.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you.
